# Do not build

These constraints override every other product instruction.

- No application, window, screen, browser-history, keystroke, mouse, or idle-time monitoring.
- No logs of who opened or viewed a file/page.
- No productivity scoring, activity leaderboards, working-hour calculations, or people tracking.
- No HR, payroll, attendance, student ERP, parent portal, or fee-management features.
- No social-feed mechanics, follower counts, likes, or popularity rankings.
- No AI auto-publishing, auto-deleting, permission changes, or final writes without a human confirmation.
- No media uploads, media storage, media library, file conversion, file
  matching, media archive, backups, storage nodes or desktop connectors.
  Chat attachments remain chat-only and are never reused by this workspace.

The workspace tracks work, never people’s activity.

## Added after the full plan review

- **No AI in a path that must be reliable.** Event history matching and direct
  work-item status are ordinary code. A model may suggest to a person; it may
  never be the thing a feature depends on. Rate limits and non-repeatable
  answers turn any dependency into half-finished work and inconsistent data.
- **No automatic creation from the school calendar.** Imported entries are
  offered; a person selects and confirms. An event nobody asked for is worse
  than no event.
- **No confidence score shown without its reason.** "96% confident" is not
  something anybody can check. Say which word matched.
- **No destructive action behind a single click.** Deleting an account
  requires the username typed out, and anything that also erases history is a
  separate, unticked choice — never the default button.
